# Budget

Budget has three connected views: Funds, Allocated Budget, and Expenses. Only Captains, Logistics & Finance, and the verified owner can open Budget or read its financial records. Other active execs see Add Expense instead; they can submit a new expense, see their own entries and reviewer feedback, and edit their own pending or changes-requested entries. They cannot view budgets, balances, ledgers, receipts, or other people's expenses. A Captain or Logistics & Finance member can review another person's expense. Finance and the verified owner can set limits and reconcile fund balances. The owner can also review, but never an expense they created or submitted. Direct table writes are denied; the database enforces permission and transitions in `0020_budget.sql`, `0021_private_budget.sql`, `0022_own_expense_edits.sql`, `0023_budget_questionnaire.sql`, `0024_budget_setup_controls.sql`, and `0025_budget_known_expense_removal.sql`.

## Money and approvals

Budget setup is a five-step questionnaire: name the season, allocate amounts to the nine standard categories and any custom categories, enter the current balances of Venmo, SOFC Good Bull and Main SOFC, list known expenses such as competition and mix fees, then review the total. The total budget is calculated from category allocations and formatted as dollars; it is never entered separately. Fund balances are actual account snapshots, not category allocations; leave an unknown balance blank. Saving a changed balance records an adjustment in that fund's history and requires all payment/refund reports for that fund to be reviewed first. Names, duplicates, amounts and totals are validated in the form, server action and database. Known expenses must fit within their category allocations and are not added again to the total. A known expense can optionally request a fund deduction: choose Venmo, Main SOFC, or Good Bull only for Hotel/Travel, then say whether the payment is already in the recorded balance. The request creates a pending expense visible in the review queue and fund ledger; a different Captain or Finance member must approve before any account balance changes. Planned-only items can be removed directly while editing the questionnaire. A known expense with a saved deduction request can be removed through the questionnaire's cancellation action; its linked expense must be canceled with a reason. Cancellation removes its planned row and reverses its approved fund effect while retaining deleted payment history privately. Revisions require a reason and preserve prior values in event history. New funds do not raise a spending limit.

Funds are Venmo, SOFC Good Bull, and Main SOFC. Good Bull can fund only Hotel or Travel expenses. Main SOFC includes football and consolidated Groupfund money. Fund balance is the latest recorded actual balance, not the spending limit. A finance/owner member may update it to match the real account balance and enter a reason; this is called reconciliation. The update is blocked while that fund has an unreviewed payment or refund report, so a pending refund must be reviewed first.

Every expense appears immediately in Expenses and in its selected fund's expandable ledger. Pending and approved unpaid portions reserve money. Approval deducts only the newly verified paid portion from the fund. A later payment or refund also needs another person's approval. If a payment or refund is already reflected in the account's recorded balance, mark it as included when reporting it; approval still records spending but leaves the balance unchanged. A compact Needs approval button at the top of Budget shows a count when submissions are pending and opens the review queue. Reviewers can approve or request changes with an explanation. Stale revisions and repeat approvals fail. Money may appear over budget or below zero; the page shows it honestly.

The overall budget circle separates verified paid, approved commitments, pending review and remaining funds. Selecting a category opens only that category’s bar graph, expenses and known costs; a back button returns to all categories. Refunds reduce net paid for budget calculations after approval. Receipt files are limited to PDFs/JPEGs/PNGs under 2 MB, stored privately and downloaded only by active budget viewers. Removing an unpaid expense without a payment report deletes its associated records. Cancelling an expense with a payment report removes it from active totals and retains the payment and cancellation history in the private database audit, hidden from app pages and exports. It reverses the expense's account-balance changes since the last reconciliation; changes already absorbed in a later recorded actual balance are not applied twice. The member Payments feature is separate and is never altered by a Budget cancellation.

The all-categories budget uses a circle graph. Individual category views and category cards use bar graphs. Funds has a segmented visual of recorded balances above its three account ledgers; a linked known expense is labeled in its fund ledger. Known expenses can be removed from Allocated Budget: planned-only rows are removed directly, while linked payment requests use the expense cancellation flow. Deleting a budget hides it from the Budget page and new expense submissions while retaining its private history; active expenses must be canceled first. Deleting a budget does not erase actual fund balance snapshots.

## Previous sheet

The previous sheet is not imported automatically. The Funds page no longer shows the old-sheet import button. Export CSV contains visible expense records only.
